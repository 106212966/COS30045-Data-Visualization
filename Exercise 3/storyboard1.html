Storyboard 1: The "Size FOMO" Reality Check

Are massive screens actually the norm? Hover over the hand-drawn graphics below to reveal the data story.

The Setup

You finally move into your new place. You go to the mall to buy a TV, and suddenly every screen is the size of a billboard. It makes you feel like a 127 cm TV is meant for ants.

The Conflict

Sales promoters make it seem like everyone is buying massive screens. You start wondering... do I really need a home cinema just to watch YouTube while eating dinner?

The Investigation

Let's ignore the showroom hype and look at the hard data. We pulled the frequency of every TV size on the market to see what real people are actually buying.

The Reveal

Look at that massive spike. The data doesn't lie—gigantic 190 cm and 216 cm TVs are extreme outliers. They aren't the norm; they are the exception.

The Insight

The 140 cm screen is the undisputed king of the living room. It completely dominates the market.

The Verdict

Don't let 'Size FOMO' get to you. A 140 cm TV isn't 'small'—it is the market standard. It fits perfectly in a standard apartment without dominating the entire room.
