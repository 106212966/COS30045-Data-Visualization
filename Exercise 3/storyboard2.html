Storyboard 2: The TNB Boss Battle

How much does sizing up actually cost you? Let's check the stats of the Small, Medium, and Large categories.

The Setup

Choosing your TV category is a lot like picking your stats. We grouped the data into three distinct tiers: Small (<44 inches), Medium (44 to 65 inches), and Large (>65 inches).

The Conflict

Everyone wants to equip the massive 'Large' tier screen, but they forget about the hidden debuff: the constant drain on your monthly electricity budget.

The Investigation

We ran the numbers to calculate the exact Average Energy Consumption (kWh/year) for each tier. Let's look at their base stats.

The Reveal

Upgrading from Small to Medium costs just a bit of extra energy. But crossing into the Large tier? The power meter goes completely into the red.

The Insight

Screen size scales aggressively. Pushing past 65 inches means exponentially more surface area to light up, dealing massive damage to your wallet.

The Verdict

The 'Medium' category (44 to 65 inches) is the ultimate meta build. It gives you maximum entertainment value without taking critical damage on your utility bill!
