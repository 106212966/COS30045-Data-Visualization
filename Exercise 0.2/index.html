Appliance Energy Consumption in the Australian Market

The Impact of Televisions on Energy Use

In Australia, household appliances account for a significant portion of daily energy consumption. While heating and cooling are major contributors, home entertainment systems—especially televisions—are becoming a growing factor in everyday power usage. With the rise of large-screen smart TVs, gaming consoles, and daily streaming, understanding how much power your television draws is more important than ever.

Choosing an energy-efficient television, guided by the mandatory Australian Energy Rating Label, can help households significantly reduce their carbon footprint and lower electricity bills without sacrificing screen size or display quality.
